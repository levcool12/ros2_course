## Conclusion (tf vs tf_static profiles)

robot_state_publisher offers tf_static as RELIABLE + TRANSIENT_LOCAL: the
publisher keeps the last message, so the late transient_local subscriber
received all three fixed transforms immediately (exit=0), while the same
late subscriber with VOLATILE durability got nothing before the timeout
(exit=124) - volatile gives no access to messages published in the past.

The /tf topic is the opposite case: it is RELIABLE + VOLATILE. That is
consistent with how the two streams differ: transforms of moving links
are produced continuously, a stale sample is useless, so there is nothing
to "latch" and a late subscriber simply takes the next live message.
Fixed transforms are published once, so transient_local durability is
what makes them available to every future subscriber.
